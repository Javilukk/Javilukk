## 🧭 Who I am

I started in **industrial automation** — PLCs, sensors, production lines — and fell in love with what you can do when you point machine learning at the data those machines produce. Now I'm building the bridge: **from the factory floor to the model**.

I taught C++ and ML at my university for three summers, and nothing exposes gaps in your understanding like explaining a Random Forest to a room of students. That habit stuck: I don't just run models, I try to be able to **explain why every decision was made** (the split, the metric, the preprocessing).

> 💡 **My rule:** if I can't explain why a number is good, it's not a result yet — it's a suspicion.
